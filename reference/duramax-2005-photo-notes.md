# 2005 Silverado 2500HD Duramax — reference photo notes

Target truck: white 2005 Chevrolet Silverado 2500HD crew cab, Duramax (LLY), 4x4, GMT800 "cat-eye".
Every photo below was visually checked against the brief before upload. Match quality and caveats are stated per photo.

## 1. Dashboard
- **01-dash-cluster-lit.jpg** — EXACT. Straight-on instrument cluster, lit amber, engine idling, from a white 2005 2500HD LS Crew Cab Duramax (Austin's Auto Sales, VIN 1GCHK23235F859657). Verifiably the diesel cluster: tach 0–5 "RPM X 1000" with redline ticks from ~3,200 rpm, transmission temperature gauge (100–300 °F) at lower left, oil pressure 0–120 psi, coolant 160–260 °F, DIC lit ("PRND321", "ODOMETER: 193469 MI").
- **01b-dash-wide-driver-pov.jpg** — EXACT, same truck and listing. The wide framing: full dash from the driver's seat, steering wheel in front, cluster lit behind it, radio/climate controls lit, headlight switch at left.

## 2. Engine bay, driver's side
- **02-engine-bay-driver-side.jpg** — EXACT (2005 2500HD LT Duramax, Victory Motors of Colorado, listing 277347 photo 43). Shot over the driver-side fender, driver side in the foreground. Visible: brake fluid reservoir (white, yellow brake-symbol cap, on the master cylinder/hydroboost at the driver-side firewall), the underhood fuse block (large black box, front driver corner), the driver-side battery (beside the fuse block; an aftermarket Duracell unit). Also: "6.6 TURBO DIESEL" cover, coolant surge tank and second battery across the bay.
- **Gap:** the washer fluid bottle is not identifiable in this or any bay photo found — on these trucks it sits low inside the driver-side fender.

## 3. Underside from the rear
- **03-underside-rear.jpg** — CLOSE match. Camera directly behind/below the rear axle: AAM rear axle/diff housing centered, spare tire under the bed on a steel wheel, exhaust curving over the axle, frame rails. Donor is a **2001 2500HD and it is lifted** (aftermarket shocks, oversized tires) — the underbody layout is GMT800-correct, the suspension hardware is not stock. Driveshaft is only visible in the distance ahead of the axle; leaf springs are not clearly readable in this frame.
- **Fuel filter note:** it cannot appear in any rear underside photo — on these trucks it mounts on the passenger-side frame rail up by the front wheel well, roughly 8 ft ahead of this view.

## 4. Hood underside
- **04-hood-underside.jpg** — EXACT part (2003–06 cat-eye HD hood, Summit White, off the truck; Rust Free Truck Parts MI). The complete molded black insulation pad with contour embossing and push-clip retainers, white inner frame stamping, latch striker at top center, hinge mounting plates at both rear corners.
- **04b-hood-underside-in-situ-hinges.jpg** — SUPPORTING shot, for the in-place hinge arrangement only: a 2004 Tahoe (earlier pre-facelift GMT800 hood variant) with the hood up, showing the hinge arms and their coil springs at both rear corners and the washer hose clipped along the hood's rear edge. Its pad is torn in the middle and the pad shape differs from the cat-eye HD hood.
- **Modeling fact:** GMT800 trucks have **no prop rod and no gas struts** — the hood is held open by spring-loaded hinges, so there is no separate support hardware to model.

## 5. Badges (05a–05c and 05e are the same white 2005 LS crew cab Duramax as shot 1)
- **05a-bowtie.jpg** — Front grille bowtie, large in the grille bar on white paint. Caveat: this emblem reads chrome/silver; the factory cat-eye bowtie insert is gold. A black hood bug deflector intrudes at the top of the crop.
- **05b-door-badge.jpg** — Front door, lower rear: the slim black/red LLY-era DURAMAX badge above the chrome 2500 HD badge (small bowtie at its left). Placement, shapes and colors are documented; lettering is soft at the 1280 px source resolution. (The legible "Allison Transmission / DURAMAX" plaque style belongs to 2006, not 2005.)
- **05c-tailgate-badges.jpg** — Chrome SILVERADO badge with bowtie, lower-left of the tailgate, crisp. **Correction to the original brief: a 2005 cat-eye tailgate has no "CHEVROLET" lettering — the Silverado badge is the only tailgate badge.**
- **05d-4x4-decal.jpg** — The correct GMT800 4x4 decal (silver italic 4s, red ×) on the rear bedside ahead of the taillight — but documented on a RED 2005 2500HD (BigIron auction gallery); it could not be found on a white truck.
- **05e-z71-decal-white.jpg** — What the exact white LS 4x4 above actually wears instead: a red/black Z71 OFF ROAD decal on the rear bedside, between the taillight and the rear wheel arch.

## 6. Stock wheel and tire
- **06-stock-wheel-tire.jpg** — EXACT fitment. Factory PY0 polished forged aluminum 16-inch 8-lug wheel, gold bowtie center cap, Michelin LTX M/S2 with the sidewall legibly reading LT245/75R16, Load Range E (Victory Motors of Colorado, 2006 2500HD LT listing 89022 — the 2006 fitment is identical to 2005 stock).

## 7. Overhead console, headliner, rear cab
- **07-overhead-console-headliner.jpg** — CLOSE match. Sharp close-up of the GMT800 overhead console in the headliner: two round map lamps (on), two black push-buttons, the open sunglasses/storage bin, headliner fabric all around. Same console unit as the 2500HD crew cab, but the donor is a 2005 Silverado 1500 regular cab (txclsx.com), so it documents the console, not the crew cab's longer headliner.
- **07b-crew-cab-rear-interior.jpg** — CLOSE match, from the exact white 2005 crew cab (same Austin's listing): view from the open rear door across the back of the cab — full headliner spanning the roof, C-pillar trim, rear bench with headrests, front seatbacks. Honest gap: no listing photo exists looking straight back at the rear wall from the front seats; the rear wall panel below the window is hidden behind the bench in every photo found (about 115 gallery photos across five GMT800 crew cab listings were reviewed).
