# Reference photo index

The game truck is a **factory-stock 2005 Silverado 2500HD LT Crew Cab, 6.5 ft box, 4WD, Duramax LLY, Summit White**.

## Main photo set: IMG_2851 to IMG_2873

These show a real **2004** truck (VIN position 10 = 4, position 8 = 1, the LB7 engine). The body, cab, bed,
color and interior are the same generation as the target 2005, so these photos are trusted for those.

**Not stock in this set, do not model:** the leveled ride height, the oversized all-terrain tires, the blue
silicone intake/intercooler boot (2864), the step bars (2868), and the spray-in bed liner (2854, 2855).
Use the factory parts instead and log anything uncertain in `ACCURACY.md`.

## LLY engine bay set: IMG_2874 to IMG_2877

Four engine bays from other trucks with the **stock LLY**. These are trusted over 2863 to 2865 for everything
on the engine: the "6.6 Turbo Diesel" cover, the factory intake tube, turbo and intercooler pipe routing,
alternator, A/C compressor, belts, oil fill, and the brake master cylinder.

## Manuals: `manuals/`

- `2005-duramax-diesel-supplement.pdf`: GM's 2005 Duramax diesel supplement (part 15101364). The source for
  the starting procedure, the Wait to Start light, elevated idle, the diesel driver information center messages,
  capacities and the engine compartment overview.
- `lly-engine-compartment-diagram.png`: the supplement's labeled engine bay drawing (page 5-14), viewed from the
  front of the truck. Trusted for the position of every labeled under-hood part.
- The general 2005 Silverado owner's manual is at
  https://assets.gm.com/manuals/chevrolet/2005_chevrolet_silverado_owners.pdf (hood release, tailgate,
  ignition positions, Tow/Haul, the cluster, axle ratio, fuel tank).
